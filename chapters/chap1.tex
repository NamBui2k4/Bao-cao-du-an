\section{Background and Rationale}
The demand for scalable real-time messaging systems has grown rapidly with the proliferation of collaborative applications, social platforms, and IoT devices \cite{guth2018comparison,tilkov2018nodejs}. Node.js, with its event-driven architecture, has become a popular choice for building such systems due to its ability to handle high concurrency and low-latency communication \cite{cantelon2017nodejs,nodejsdocs}. WebSocket technology further enables persistent, bidirectional communication channels, which are essential for instant message delivery and interactive user experiences \cite{brown2019websockets,baker2017websocket}. Recent advances in cloud infrastructure and managed services, such as AWS, have made it feasible to deploy and scale these systems globally \cite{awsdocs,roberts2020aws}.
